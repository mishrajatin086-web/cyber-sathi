NEED HELP?

If You Think You Got Scammed

1

Stop communication

Scammer ko reply mat karo aur suspicious link dobara open mat karo.

2

Secure your account

Password change karo aur affected account par MFA enable karo.

3

Contact official support

Bank, payment app ya service ki official website/app se contact karo.

4

Save evidence and report

Screenshots, numbers aur transaction details save karke report karo.
